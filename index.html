§ 01 — RESEARCH

Four field cases

One question runs through all of them: how much can a model tell us about a place in the hours after it has been hit?

CASE 01

Height-aware building damage assessment

Does knowing how tall a building was before the event help a model judge what happened to it? I fuse pre-event LiDAR heights (USGS 3DEP) with xBD imagery inside a MambaBDA backbone to find out.

LiDARxBDState-space modelsMultimodal fusion
In preparation · Journal
CASE 02

II-DAMNet: seeing disaster at night

Drones fly when they can, not when the light is good. II-DAMNet translates night-time UAV imagery into the day domain so segmentation models trained on daylight scenes keep working after dark.

UAVDomain translationSemantic segmentation
Published · IEEE AVSS
CASE 03

AnthropEco-Net: damage beyond buildings

A multi-task network that segments ecological and human-made damage together, so forests, fields and waterways show up in the assessment alongside roofs and roads.

Multi-task learningEcologyUAV
Journal version · In progress
CASE 04

AIO-FLOOD: from maps to evacuation

Coupling AI & optimization to plan flood evacuations: damage and exposure estimates feed routing and shelter decisions, closing the loop between perception and action.

OptimizationFloodsDecision support
Funding proposal · ANR JCJC
