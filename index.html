§ 01 — RESEARCH

Three field cases

One question runs through all of them: how much can a model tell us about a place in the hours after it has been hit?

CASE 01
Published · IEEE AVSS 2026

II-DAMNet: seeing disaster at night

Drones fly when they can, not when the light is good. II-DAMNet translates night-time UAV imagery into the day domain so segmentation models trained on daylight scenes keep working after dark.

CASE 02
Published · AI4Nature @ AVSS 2026

HMDAB-Net: water and wreckage in one map

Floods and storms make up nearly three quarters of recent disasters, yet most models see either the water or the damage. HMDAB-Net runs two specialised streams — a U-Net for flood extent and a GA-optimised PSPNet for damaged buildings and blocked roads — then cleans and merges their masks with a priority-based late-fusion rule.

CASE 03
Published · Artificial Evolution (EA 2026)

EDCNet: evolving networks small enough to fly

Damage maps are needed on the drone, not in a data centre. A genetic algorithm searches for compact U-shaped encoder–decoders under strict parameter and FLOP budgets. The evolved EDCNet stays within about five mIoU points of PSPNet on rescue-critical classes — with 13× fewer parameters and roughly three times the speed.
